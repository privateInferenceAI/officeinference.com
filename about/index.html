About Office Inference

A one-person AI shop in Colebrook.

I help small firms in Litchfield County and the Hartford area get value from AI they already own. Most of the work is the last mile: permissions, documents, integration, training and a way to prove it worked.

Why this exists

Small firms around here get sold AI by people who never have to make it work. The demo is easy. The rollout is not, and the invoice arrives either way.

I started Office Inference to be the person who shows up after the purchase and tells the truth about it. Sometimes that means fixing the deployment. Sometimes it means saying stop and saving the next licence renewal.

What I built

I did not start with a slide deck. I built and run a private AI document assistant: cited answers, approval gates, an audit record of every answer, and tested restores. I wrote a 43-chapter guide to the business AI market and audited every component in my own stack.

I show the records on the call. If I am asking you to trust the work, you should be able to see mine.

How I work

  • Fixed scope and a price before work starts.
  • No commissions from Microsoft or any vendor. You buy licences in your own name.
  • Written findings you keep, whether or not you hire me.
  • If the answer is stop, I say stop.
  • Support after go-live, if you want it, on a monthly service level.

What I don't do

  • Sell licences or take referral fees.
  • Promise a result I cannot measure.
  • Give legal advice. I build controls and evidence; your counsel decides obligations.
  • Take over your IT provider's job. I work alongside them.

Where I work

Litchfield County in person, including Colebrook, Torrington, Winsted, Litchfield, New Hartford, Salisbury, Lakeville, Kent and New Milford. Hartford and West Hartford in person. The rest of Connecticut by video, because most of this work is documents, permissions and training rather than hardware.

Office Inference is based in Colebrook, Connecticut. Reach me at anthony@officeinference.com.
