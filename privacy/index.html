Privacy

What I do with your details

This site has two forms: one to request the free call, one to get the checklist. Both ask for your name and email. The call form also asks for your firm, an optional phone number, and what you tried.

How it is used

  • To reply to you and set up the call.
  • To send the checklist and the one follow-up message, if you ask for the call.
  • Nothing else. I do not add you to a newsletter and I do not sell or share your details.

Who processes it

The forms are handled by Formspree, which forwards the submission to my email. Formspree processes the data on its own infrastructure. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and sets no cookies of its own.

What this site does not do

No analytics, no advertising pixels, no tracking cookies, no third-party fonts or scripts. The only script on the site copies campaign tags from the address bar into the form so I know which ad you came from. It runs in your browser and sends nothing on its own.

Keeping it

I keep enquiries while we are talking and for up to twelve months afterward, so I can follow up if you ask me to. You can ask me to delete your details at any time by emailing anthony@officeinference.com.
