About

At Office Inference we build private AI systems for small businesses, in the cloud or in your office, and we do the whole job: the hardware, the software, the install, the training.

Where we work

We're based in Colebrook, a small town in northwest Connecticut. Cloud and Dedicated Cloud builds happen mostly remotely. In-Office installs happen at your site, on your hardware. If you've got a network connection and a spot to rack a server, distance doesn't matter much.
