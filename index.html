AI that answers questions about your business, from your own files.

Some businesses need everything to stay in the building. Some don't care. We build the same system all three ways.

Office Inference Cloud

No hardware required.

You get the same chat, the same document search, the same automation. The only difference: the model doing the thinking is a commercial one from a frontier provider. For businesses without strict data rules.

Office Inference Dedicated

Your cloud account, under your contracts.

The same system, deployed into a cloud account you own. Your documents stay inside your own tenancy, under the compliance agreements your cloud provider offers, encrypted at rest. Regulated work gets its paperwork satisfied without a server in the building.

Office Inference In-Office

Your hardware, your office.

Everything runs on hardware you own. Your staff chats with the system, the system reads your files, and none of it goes anywhere near the internet. Built for businesses where a leaked document isn't an embarrassment, it's a lawsuit. The trade-off is real: you buy the server, you maintain it, and the largest models won't fit on it.

Which one fits

If your documents have rules attached (patient records, privileged files, or financial data), you can't just hand them to a public chatbot. But you still need the advantages AI provides, so we build the system that lets you have it.

Cloud is the right answer when your documents are ordinary business material and you want the lowest cost and the fastest start.

Dedicated is where most regulated work lands. Your documents stay in your own tenancy, the compliance agreements come from your cloud provider, and you pay monthly instead of buying hardware.

In-Office is the right answer when you want the data physically incapable of leaving the building, when your internet is unreliable, or when you would rather own the infrastructure. Know the trade-offs going in: you buy the server, you maintain it, and the largest models don't fit on it.

If you're not sure, email us. We'll tell you which one fits, and we'll say so when the answer is that you don't need us.

How it works

It's not magic, it's more like a layered cake. One slice reads your documents and indexes them. One slice decides which AI model answers what, and logs it. One slice is the chat you actually see. One slice does the grunt work: email drafts, approval routing, reminders. In-Office means all of it runs in your building. Dedicated means all of it runs in a cloud account you own. Cloud means the thinking part is handled by a frontier provider.

Start where you are. Move when the rules change.

Cloud is where most clients start because it's the most economical in the beginning. Dedicated is where regulated work usually lands, because the paperwork gets satisfied without buying hardware. In-Office is where clients end up when they want the data physically incapable of leaving, or when the monthly bills pass what a server would have cost. All three run the same system, so moving is a deployment change, not a rebuild.
