It is usually not the model

The software generally works. The rollout is what fails. These are the four things I find most often.

The seats are paid for

Licences renew every month and almost nobody logs in. The invoice is the only proof the purchase happened.

The pilot never shipped

It worked in the demo and died in the workflow. Nobody owned the move to production.

The documents are a mess

Scans, duplicates and unknown permissions. The assistant answers from the wrong copy or refuses.

Nobody changed the work

No training, no measurement, no reason to stop using email. People went back to what they knew.
