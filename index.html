AI that answers questions about your business, from your own files.

Some businesses need everything to stay in the building. Some don't care. We build the same system both ways.

Office Inference Private

Your hardware, your office.

Everything runs on hardware you own. Your staff chats with the system, the system reads your files, and none of it goes anywhere near the internet. Built for businesses where a leaked document isn't an embarrassment, it's a lawsuit.

Office Inference Cloud

No hardware required.

You get the same chat, the same document search, the same automation. The only difference: the model doing the thinking is a commercial one we reach over the internet. For businesses without strict data rules.

Start where you are. Move when the rules change.

Cloud is where most clients start because it's more economical in the beginning. When the API bill gets ugly or you need physical control of your data, the move to Private is a hardware swap to a machine in your office. The same system works at your office with a quick switch to a self-hosted LLM.

Who it's for

If your documents have rules attached (patient records, privileged files, or financial data), you can't just hand them to a public chatbot. But you still need the advantages AI provides, so we build the private system that lets you have it.

How it works

It's not magic, it's more like a layered cake. One slice reads your documents and indexes them. One slice decides which AI model answers what, and logs it. One slice is the chat you actually see. One slice does the grunt work: email drafts, approval routing, reminders. Private means all of it runs in your office. Cloud means the thinking part is rented.
